Common Questions

Everything you need to know

Is hypnotherapy safe?

Yes, completely. Clinical hypnotherapy is a natural, relaxed state — similar to daydreaming. You remain fully aware and in control at all times. You cannot be made to do or say anything against your will.

How many sessions will I need?

This varies by individual and presenting issue. Many people notice significant improvements within 4–8 sessions. We'll discuss a realistic timeline during your free consultation.

Do you offer online sessions?

Yes. I offer both in-person and online sessions via video call, making therapy accessible wherever you are in the UK.

What's the difference between CBT and hypnotherapy?

CBT works consciously — examining and restructuring thoughts and behaviours. Hypnotherapy works with the subconscious. Used together, they address both the rational and deeper emotional aspects of change.

How much do sessions cost?

Session fees are detailed during your free consultation. I aim to keep therapy accessible, and payment plans may be available. Please get in touch to discuss.

Is everything confidential?

Absolutely. Everything discussed in sessions is strictly confidential, in line with professional ethical guidelines. The only exceptions are where there is a risk of serious harm.
